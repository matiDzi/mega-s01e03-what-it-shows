Shared parser reads day-first dates as month-first

The shared parser treats the month as the first part of every date. For tenants whose locale writes the day first, the parser can swap the day and month. This group covers the remaining app and email reports, and day-first tenant form reports from booking-v2. 374 reports.

Booking form hint conflicts with tenant date order

The booking-v3 form tells people to enter dates month first, but the form reads them using the tenant's day-first locale. A form report from a booking-v3 tenant therefore has a different cause from a parser report, even though both can show a swapped date. 33 reports.
